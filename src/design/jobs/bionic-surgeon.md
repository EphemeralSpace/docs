# Bionic Surgeon

{{#template ../../templates/implemented.md}}

The Bionic Surgeon is a **Medical** job who excels in quick surgical procedures and is the only one who can safely operate on patients to install their Bionic Transplants.
As well as this, the Bionic Surgeon can attempt to surgically remove worms from a patient. This process is always fatal to the subject.

## We Can Rebuild Them!

The Bionic Surgeon's medical locker has a portable freezer containing three Bionic Transplants.
Bionic Transplants are the latest frontier in biotechnology, and the culmination of research into biotechnologies that this research vessel has been working on. Typically, they're used in monkey experiments.

Bionic Transplants can only be applied by the Bionic Surgeon. This risky surgery requires the patient to be on an operating table to function.
A transplant has both an immediate benefit to the patient, and permanently alters their biology, with positives and drawbacks to receiving the transplant.

The Bionic Surgeon's portable freezer contains the following:
- Hardened Skin. This skin transplant replaces all damaged external tissues, recovering a chunk of most forms of damage when transplanted. This skin replacement raises the recipient's HP by a bit once transplanted, but makes their skin tone bright red.
- Bionic Lungs. This lung transplant will replace smoke damaged lungs, curing smoke inhalation instantly. Bionic Lungs naturally filter air breathed in, granting the effects of a gas mask against dangerous gases, but cause the wearer to wheeze when talking.
- Iron Stomach. Removes un-metabolized chemicals when transplanted. Having an Iron Stomach allows you to swallow items into an internal inventory, or retch them back out. When you go into a critical state, you spew out everything in your internal storage involuntarily.


## Surgical Malpractice

A few cuts in the wrong places can leave a patient far worse off than before.
As an action, the Bionic Surgeon can choose a type of injury and then operate on a patient who is in a critical state, restrained on a bed, or laying on a surgery table.
If the doafter is successful, the Surgeon inflicts their chosen injury on the patient.
This can be used to hobble someone to keep them on a short leash, cause the rapid deterioration of health in someone who was seeking helpful treatment... Whatever you think justifies making Hippocrates hang his head in shame.
