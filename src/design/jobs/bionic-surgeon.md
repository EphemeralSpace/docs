# Bionic Surgeon

{{#template ../../templates/implemented.md}}

The Bionic Surgeon is a **Medical** job who excells in quick surgical procedures and is the only one who can safely operate on patients to install their Bionic Transplants.
As well as this, the Bionic Surgeon can attempt to surgically remove worms from a patient. This process is always fatal to the subject.

## We Can Rebuild Them!

The Bionic Surgeon's medical locker has a portable freezer containing three Bionic Transplants.
Bionic Transplants are the latest frontier in biotechnology, and the culmination of research into biotechnologies that this research vessel has been working on. Typically, they're used in monkey experiments.

Bionic Transplants can only be applied by the Bionic Surgeon. This risky surgery requires the patient to be on an operating table to function.
A transplant has both an immidiate beneift to the patient, and permanantly alters their biology, with positives and drawbacks to recieving the transplant.

The Bionic Surgeon's portable freezer contains the following:
- Hardened Skin. This skin transplant replaces all damaged external tissues, recovering a chunk of mostforms of damage when transplanted. This skin replacement raises the recipent's HP by a bit once transplanted, but makes their skin tone bright red.
- Bionic Lungs. This lung transplant will replace smoke damaged lungs, curing smoke inhalation instantly. Bionic Lungs naturally filter air breathed in, granting the effects of a gas mask against dangerous gasses, but cause the wearer to wheeze when talking.
- Iron Stomach. Removes unmetabolized chemicals when transplanted. Having an Iron Stomach allows you to swallow items into an internal inventory, or retch them back out. When you go into a critical state, you spew out everythng in your internal storage involentarily.


## Risky Surgery

Someone as expert at surgeries and the body as the Bionic Surgeon can not just add to the body, but attempt to remove from it.
If a patient has worm parasites, or specific death-activated implants, the Bionic Surgeon can attempt to guess which one is present in the body they are operating on the surgical table, and attempt to defuse the biological mechanisms which make them functional in the way that a bomb squad might defuse a bomb.
Right or wrong, this process is fatal to the patient, but guessing correctly will stop a burrowed worm, implanted bomb or cryonics mechanism from triggering on that death.
Guessing wrong is VERY bad for the surgeon, giving no delay before activating the death effect and putting them directly in the area of effect.
