# Botanist

{{#template ../../templates/implemented.md}}

The Botanist manages the station's garden and greenhouse, and can grow food as well as having the unique ability to crossbreed plants.
Botanists use the same botany system as everyone else, but are able to go further beyond the average crew member's interaction with it.

## Green Thumb

Botanists are able to easily tell the conditions of any plants growing, even in soil without fancy computers.
While the garden is a public space, when things go south with hungry animals or rampant weeds, their custodial access also allows them to acquire more weed killer and pesticides.

The Botanist's trusty shovel can dig a new plot to grow plants on any patch of soil in the station, but it isn't at all unique to them.
While not at all advised by ship engineers, the Botanist's locker contains bags of soil which they can use in order to to turn regular hull plating tiles on the station into fertile ground to grow plants on.

```admonish tip "Note about Botany Plants"
Botany's plant options should be greatly improved from what they currently are, to tie into more systems than just providing food. Thorny thickets that slow movement, beautiful flowers which raise moods and pest-repellent hedges are all potential avenues for expanding the uses of botany to make them more than a supplier for the kitchen and bar.
```

## Crossbreeding

Botanists have the unique ability to sense a Botanical Trait inside a plant. Botanical Traits are assigned randomly at the start of each round, so the botanist has to grow the plants to observe their traits.
Botanists can use their ability to combine three plants. If they have the correct three traits, they can synthesize the seeds to a more dramatic and powerful plant.
Advanced plants made in this way have no Botanical Traits, and can't be upgraded further.
