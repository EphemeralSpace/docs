# Firefighter

The Firefighter is a role focused on station atmospheric maintenance, specifically putting out fires and clearing lingering smoke.
When the station is ablaze, they're the individual most ready to put out the flames with their advanced equipment, giving them a unique leg up.
Additionally, their fireaxe (specialized for breaching) and light repair tools give them the ability to both break into and fix up areas affected by fires.

## Hot Fashion

The firefighter's clothes consist of soot-gray overalls with red and yellow accents.
Unlike a proper engineer, they are less of a handyman and more of a rough-and-tumble thrill seeker ready to throw themselves into an inferno.
They also keep a firesuit inside their locker, although this is more of a convenience and isn't distinguished from other firesuits littered around the station.

The most striking part of the firefighter's appearance is their gas mask, being directly on their face at the start of the shift.
Due to the lingering trauma from lifelong smoke inhalation, the firefighter gains a moderate amount of [fear](../emotions/fear.md) when not wearing a gas mask of some kind.
This gives them a suspicious and threatening appearance, since the gas mask obscures their features and hides their voice.

## Pyroeuphoria

The main tool at the firefighter's disposal for dealing with large fires is the **backpack extinguisher**.
It consists of two parts: a large tank worn on the back as well as an attached extinguisher that can be brought in and out from the tank via an action.
When the tank is worn, the attached extinguisher can be used indefinitely without needing to be refilled with water.

The secondary tool at the firefighter's disposal is their **pyroeuphoria**.
When on fire, they will rapidly build the [euphoria](../emotions/euphoria.md) emotion, countering the downsides of their various fire equipment.
This means that, despite the downsides that typically come from wearing various firefighting equipment, the firefighter is better suited to dealing with these due to their emotional high.
Even more, the strong effects of pyroeuphoria enable them to make dangerous plays by leveraging the buffs they gain while on fire.
