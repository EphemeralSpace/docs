# Ecologist

{{#template ../../templates/implemented.md}}

The Ecologist is a **Custodial** and **Science** job that manages the station's artificial habitats, including the public gardens, experiment enclosures and offworld ecology research.
Their duties involve tidying, trimming, weeding, caring for animals, and maintaining the fine balance of the stations ecosystems. They're somewhat of a jack of all trades around the station.
Their trusty hardhat, rake and hedge trimmers help out with this, but their true talents come from years of experience with the strange things they find around the station.

## Veterinarian Care

The Ecologist, in addition to their groundskeeping tools, has access to the Veterinarian Ward to heal and operate on sick or injured pets from the station. The ward comes with a full set of Surgical Tools, along with first aid supplies.
In a pinch, the ward can be used to operate on people as well, but the Veterinarian Surgical Table has a reduced efficiency when used by people.
Should a pet somehow be under the control of a player (e.g. through [Reincarnation](../reincarnation.md)), the Ecologist can also Autopsy dead pets to learn their identity, using their own autopsy scalpel. They lack the expertise to autopsy humans this way.

## Identifying Eye

Strange plants and fungi can be found all over the station. Some might grow in filth, others might grow on fungal creep, some naturally grow in maintenance and on soil in the station's artificial habitats.
At the start of each round, the effects of these plants are randomized, but the Ecologist is able to tell at a glance what effects they have, fully aware of the finer details of the station ecology.

## Gentle Touch

A true ecologist is dedicated to preserving natural environments, no matter how dangerous they may be, and that means being able to safely interact with them without raising hostility from the life that calls that habitat home.
The Ecologist won't cause any aggression from natural threats on the station, such as wild animals, pests, or ferrofungus, unless the Ecologist exhibits threat by entering the harm stance near to them.
Due to this they can traverse hazardous areas for loot or save crew members from dangerous situations without needing to engage in combat.
