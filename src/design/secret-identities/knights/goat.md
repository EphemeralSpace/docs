# Concept

A ghastly helmet in the image of a goat, electricity conducting between its horns. The crest of the Goat is the master of electrokinesis, utilizing their psionic powers for shock and awe. They're strongest in combat and great at breaking, entering, and getting the fuck out.
The Goat Crest is a front-line warrior and can get other knights in and out of locations in team-up events. Their best chance at stealth is simply beating anyone who might see them revert to normal to death before leaving, and most people will be too scared to even be in beating range to begin with.

# Psionic Charge

The Goat Crest must drain energy from an APC or substation in order to gain the Psionic Charge needed to enter Aberrant form. An APC grants one pip of Psionic Charge and briefly causes an outage in connected devices, whereas draining a substation grants all five pips but cuts the power to all connected devices for a minute long time.

# Aberrant Form

Along with the standard Armor, the Goat Crest gains the Thunder Hammer weapon, which does hefty blunt damage, causes knockback, and can be thrown to do the same at range, returning after it has been thrown.
The Goat Crest gains the ability to release bolts of lighting in Aberrant Form, damaging anyone in the line of fire with shock damage and causing doors and other electronic to malfunction, bursting doors open and switching devices on and off. This ability has a 10 second cooldown.

# Lightning Anomaly

The Lightning Anomaly must be placed on top of wires. It drains power from the grid while connected to it, gaining in strength over time, and lashing out with bolts of lightning to anyone foolish enough to get nearby. The only way to get rid of it is to cut the power to it, but this causes it to rapidly diminish.
