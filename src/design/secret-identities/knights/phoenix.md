# Concept

The unstoppable Phoenix is the most survivable of the Knights, able to spring back to life when entering braincrit, but has less abilities in Aberrant Form compared to the other Crests.
Their ability to ace as a Psuedo-Jester makes them able to bluff and bait people into dangerous situations, like a worm might, before turning the tables and killing their would-be slayer.
Unlike a worm, they're able to survive environmental hazards this way too.
As their anomaly makes fire, they can regain charge from their own anomaly, also making them one of the fastest charging Crests, potentially. Despite this, using charge wildly might trade your chance at bursting back to life later.

# Psionic Charge

Using their ability to drain charge, the Phoenix can absorb fire from a tile, extinguishing and nearby fire tiles it and gaining charge in the process if 5 or more fires are extinguished by the ability.

# Aberrant Form

The Phoenix Armor has full fire immunity, and provides the Phoenix Crest Knight with an infinite supply of feather-like throwing knives to attack with.
If the Phoenix Crest Knight has full charge and is put into a critical condition, they automatically enter Aberrant Form, fully heal from all injuries and pain, and explode in a small fiery burst while doing so.

# Fire Anomaly

The Fire Anomaly scorches the land around it, creating fire and smoke constantly without ever burning out. Spraying or filling it with water will extinguish it, while feeding it with fuel or other burnable items makes it stronger.
