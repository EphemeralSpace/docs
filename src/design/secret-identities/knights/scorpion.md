# Concept

The Scorpion Crest is the "Assassin" piece of the Knights. They have the unique ability to set up an ambush and can be lethal to crew in situations where they don't get instant medical help.
The vile Scorpion crest can assassinate with lasting injures and pain, avoiding direct confrontation and letting their venom work for them.

# Psionic Charge

Eating pills, pest corpses, worms and fungus all restore charge pips for the Scorpion Crest.

# Aberrant Form

The Scorpion Crest armor grants invisibility while standing still, making it usable for ambush. While invisible, the remaining use time on the armor drains 1/10th of the normal rate.
The armor's main weapon are two claws which crush and pierce at close range.
However, the Scorpion Crest, once per transformation, can also lash out with a poison tail, injecting their target with a fatal reagent that does a tiny bit of poison damage each second for a long duration, putting them on a deadly timer to seek medical attention and have the toxins purified.

# Blight Anomaly

The Blight anomaly is invisible, detectable only by its effect of causing increasing Fear the longer crew are near it. Experiencing euphoria near it will suppress it and reveal it while a euphoric person is nearby. Pain nearby to it empowers it further, intensifying the fear.
