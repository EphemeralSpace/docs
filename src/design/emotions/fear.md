# Fear

{{#template ../../templates/unimplemented.md}}

Fear is an [emotion](../emotions.md) which represent an internal anxiety or strong terror response to something dangerous or frightening.
In gameplay, it serves as a combat disincentive that simultaneously extends combats encounters while incentivizing fleeing.

Fear arises most naturally as a response to pain, mostly from being attacked.
Characters with certain traits like **Phobias** may also experience fear as a response to being nearby specific triggers.
Consuming various chemical depressants--commonly, alcohol; uncommonly, sedatives and benzos--can help reduce fear, although it naturally deteriorates over time.

## Effects

As a general condition, fear worsens combat capabilities while improving movement speed.
Melee damage and weapon accuracy drastically declines while movement speed rises, making escaping from a general attacker easier.
At high levels of fear, characters begin to stutter while speaking and may struggle to physically fight at all.
