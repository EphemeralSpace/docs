# Euphoria

{{#template ../../templates/unimplemented.md}}

Euphoria is an [emotion](../emotions.md) that represents a vast surge of energy that overwhelms physical feeling.
While euphoric, an individual may seem capable of superhuman feats of strengths, however this is simply a facade that falls away with the emotion.

Like most emotions, Euphoria can be induced by drugs, but also through intense mental responses like the [firefighter's pyroeuphoria](../jobs/firefighter.md).

## Effects

Euphoria is a condition which "hides" various negative conditions while active.
Pain, slowdown, and various debuffs become inactive while euphoric.
Additionally, euphoric players have an increased movement speed, field of vision, and melee speed.
Once it wears off, however, they gain a debuff which slows all of those aspects for a period of time.
