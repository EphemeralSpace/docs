> **Organizations:** [Crew](../secret-identities/crew.md), [Traitors](../secret-identities/traitors.md), [Knights](../secret-identities/knights.md)
>
> **Description:** The crew must protect the King in order to foil the plans of both Knights and Traitors.

## Concept
Protect the King turns one Nobleman into the most important objective on the ship. The masquerade always guarantees a Nobleman generates who is always guaranteed to be a additional kill goal for the Traitors and an additional ancestor of all Knights that they have to kill.
To protect the king, two Avengers are also guaranteed to spawn with the Ex-Syndicate modifier.

## Gameplay
The crew have an obvious keystone to rally around, but none of the roles involved in this arrangement are confirmable, making it fairly easy to fake and thus making it easy for crew to become suspicious of them.
Traitors and Knights may enter a shaky alliance to complete their shared goal, but it is VERY shaky.
Ultimately, if one wins, the other can't, meaning this mode can just as easily bring those two organizations into direct conflict as they attempt to ensure the other won't win first.
