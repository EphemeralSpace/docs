> **Name:** Night of Knights
>
> **Organizations:** [Crew](../secret-identities/crew.md), [Knights](../secret-identities/knights.md)
>
> **Description:** A high number of Knights-Aberrant assail the station, creating a chaotic round.

## Concept
Pretty much the default for Knights, with a composition not that different to standard traitors, except Knights replace the traitors.
While the average knight can be a bit more disruptive than a traitor with their hit and run tactics, they're pretty much interchangeable for the purpose of a lot of masquerades.
Thus, this one is no different to the typical traitors mode, except with traitor slots filled with Knights instead.

## Gameplay
While the composition is the same between Night of Knights and traitors, the rounds will feel very different. The high number of Knights allows for brazen group hit and run attacks, and the anomalies gives crew a lot of fires to put out.
At least it won't be ambiguous what the threats are and where, but there will always be the doubt that it could still be another type of Knight round.
